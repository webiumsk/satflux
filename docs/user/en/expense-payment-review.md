# Review expense payments and duplicates

## Match a payment to an existing expense

In your company's **Payments**, choose the expense matching action on an unmatched
outgoing movement. Review the suggested expense and confirm the association.
Candidates must match the full positive amount, currency and a strong variable
symbol or document reference. Partial payments, overpayments and unverified email
payment directions are not eligible. If several expenses match, choose the correct
one after checking the documents.

Confirmation marks the existing expense paid using the bank booking date; it does
not create another expense. A stale or conflicting association is rejected. Refresh
the list and review the current data before trying again.

Removing the association leaves the expense's payment status unchanged. Review the
expense separately if its payment status also needs correcting.

## Possible duplicate expenses

When entering or editing an expense, review any possible duplicate warnings. They
compare the document number, amount and currency, or the title, received date, amount
and currency. They do not verify that the suppliers are the same.

Open the suggested existing record to check it. A warning does not merge or delete
anything and does not prevent saving a legitimate separate expense.

Local-first invoicing calculates these suggestions on your device without sending
your local expense data to the server.

After mapping spreadsheet columns in **Import expenses**, the preview also checks
existing expenses and repeated rows inside the file. It shows up to twenty advisory
warnings. Review them before importing; similarity alone does not skip a row or
change the existing internal-number conflict rules. A failed duplicate check is
reported separately and does not mean that no duplicates exist.
