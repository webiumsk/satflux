# Accept cards with Stripe

> When [temporary free PRO access](/documentation/free-pro-access) is active, registered non-guest accounts with a verified email can use PRO features without a subscription purchase. The usual limits and ownership permissions still apply. Check Pricing and Account for the current mode.

Alongside Bitcoin and Lightning, you can accept **card payments** through **Stripe**. This is a Pro feature.

## Set it up

1. Open your store and go to **Stripe**.
2. Connect your Stripe account / enter your Stripe credentials as prompted.
3. Enable Stripe as a payment method.

Once connected, customers can choose to pay by card at checkout in addition to your Bitcoin options.

## Notes

- Stripe requires **PRO access** (see [Plans & limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits)).
- Card payments settle to your Stripe account, not your Bitcoin wallet - they are a separate rail you control.
- You can turn individual payment methods on or off in the store's payment settings.
