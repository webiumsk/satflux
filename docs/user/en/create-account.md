# Create your Satflux account

> When [temporary free PRO access](/documentation/free-pro-access) is active, registered non-guest accounts with a verified email can use PRO features without a subscription purchase. The usual limits and ownership permissions still apply. Check Pricing and Account for the current mode.

You can start using Satflux in under a minute - no email required.

## Sign up with a recovery phrase

1. Open the **Register** page and choose **Create account with recovery phrase**.
2. Satflux generates a **24-word recovery phrase**. Write it down in order and keep it somewhere safe and offline.
3. Confirm that you have saved the phrase. If a human-verification check appears, complete it to create the account and sign in.

> Your recovery phrase backs up your Satflux account, your BTCPay store, and your local invoicing data. It is the only way to restore access - **Satflux does not store it and cannot recover it for you.**

## Verification during signup

When signup protection is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks the creation of a new account after you confirm your phrase backup. Your recovery phrase is not submitted to the verification service. If verification expires or fails, use the retry button; the confirmed phrase stays the same while you keep this registration page open. Keep your written backup. Existing account sign-in and recovery do not require this signup check.

## Add a passkey (recommended)

Right after signing up, Satflux offers to add a **passkey**. A passkey lets you sign in on this device with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of typing the 24 words. You can add or remove passkeys later in your account settings.

## Add an email (optional)

You can add an email address in your account later. It is optional for getting started, but adding and verifying it is required for promotional PRO access when the promotion is active. It is also used for billing, notifications and confirming sensitive changes, never as your login secret.

1. Open **Account** and enter your email in the **Upgrade guest account** box (or use the upgrade prompt that appears when a feature needs an email).
2. We email you a **6-digit code**. Type it into the form - the code expires after 10 minutes and works only once.
3. Done. Nothing changes on your account until the code is confirmed, so an unconfirmed address never gets in the way.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or press **Send a new code** (available again after a short countdown). After five wrong attempts the code is locked - request a new one. If you typed the address wrong, choose **Use a different email address** and start again.
