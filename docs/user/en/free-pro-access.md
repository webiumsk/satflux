# Temporary free PRO access

When the free PRO promotion is active, eligible accounts receive PRO features **free of charge, until further notice**. The Pricing page and your Account show whether the promotion is active. This article describes that mode; it does not announce that it has already started.

## Who receives access

You need a **registered account with a verified email address**. An account that is still a guest is not eligible, even if you signed in using a recovery phrase or passkey.

If you started without email, open **Account**, add your email through the guest-account upgrade form and confirm the emailed code. Adding an address without confirming it is not enough. Your recovery phrase and passkeys remain your sign-in methods. See [Create your account](/documentation/create-account).

Existing manually assigned PRO access keeps its current validity. Enterprise access and administrative permissions do not change.

## Features and limits

Eligible accounts can use the current PRO features, including business invoicing, exports, advanced statistics, Stripe and supported cash/card Point of Sale functions. Free access does not connect a wallet or a Stripe account for you; follow the usual setup instructions. Charges from Stripe or other providers still apply under their terms.

The promotion uses the usual PRO limits:

| Item | Standard PRO allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Stores | 3 per account |
| Store API keys | 3 active keys per store |
| Panel API keys | 3 active keys per account |
| Ticket events | 3 per store |
| Owned invoicing companies | 2, plus existing additional slots or applicable account allowances |
| Hosted Lightning addresses and raffles | No PRO count limit |
| ISDOC extractions | Existing base allowance of 20 per account, plus previously purchased credits |

Check the actual allowance shown in your account and on each feature page. The promotion does not reset used ISDOC extractions or give unlimited extraction. Companies shared with you do not count as companies you own.

PRO access does not give you Enterprise store-user management or access to someone else's stores or companies. Existing invitations, roles and ownership still apply.

## Purchases and billing

While this mode is active, **new PRO subscriptions, trials and PRO subscription-credit purchases are paused**. New purchases of company slots and ISDOC extraction packs are also paused. Enterprise billing remains unchanged.

Free access does not create a subscription, use your trial, change your role or automatically charge you. Your existing billing history, purchased slots and unused credits are retained. Account may therefore show a Free billing plan alongside available PRO features; these describe different things.

If you reach a limit, you cannot buy a paused add-on to raise it. Existing objects above the current limit are retained; creating more remains subject to the applicable allowance. An exhausted ISDOC allowance can use existing purchased credits, but no new packs can be bought.

## Duration and future pricing

There is **no scheduled end date**. “Until further notice” does not mean lifetime access. A return to paid PRO would require a separate announcement and published terms. The promotion does not automatically turn into a paid subscription.

Keep your recovery phrase backed up and use the usual [backup and recovery](/documentation/backup-and-recovery) guidance. See [Plans and limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits) for the underlying plans.
