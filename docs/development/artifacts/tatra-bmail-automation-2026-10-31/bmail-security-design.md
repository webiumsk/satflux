# Automatic Tatra B-mail: independent security design

Reviewer: `sepa247_security`, independent read-only design review, 2026-10-31. Observed consumer base `cd951b3b`. No source, Git, service, account or deployment changes were made for this review.

## Decision

Automatic confirmation for a precisely supported Tatra notification subset is viable. Blanket manual-only handling was a temporary quarantine while provenance was missing, not a fundamental conclusion about bank email and not completion of the automatic-confirmation product goal.

The published historical templates support an anchored prefix with a credited-account statement, three numeric balances, a constrained transaction-code/bank-account description and then the bank's reference field. Free recipient text or a counterparty name follows the reference. A full recognizer for that structure can distinguish the reference position from fabricated labels in later remittance. Missing current samples limit coverage claims; they do not inherently make that historical subset structurally unsafe. [ISPadmin's primary Tatra integration example](https://wiki4.ispadmin.eu/cz/faq/centralni-sklad/360-cz/fakturace/menu-fakturace.html), [REMP's raw fixture, independently found by the researcher](https://github.com/remp2020/crm-payments-module/blob/master/docs/tatrabanka.eml).

The current bank page corroborates constrained transaction codes and numeric bank/account descriptions, but does not provide a complete current email grammar. Treat the two historical examples as explicit versioned format evidence, not a promise of universal or permanent bank compatibility. [Tatra B-mail documentation](https://www.tatrabanka.sk/sk/personal/ucet-platby/internet-banking/b-mail/).

## Correct the earlier threat interpretation

A payer is expected to choose the invoice's QR/E2E/VS reference. If genuine funds reach the correct merchant account in the required currency/amount and are applied exactly once to that uniquely selected invoice, choosing another invoice is not, by itself, a financial exploit or authorization bypass. Third-party payment of an invoice is normal.

My earlier 30 assertions established that a body label affected reference selection. They did not establish theft, fabricated credit or underpayment when one real credit is applied once. The broad reference-ownership framing was overconstrained. The concrete remaining concerns are ambiguity between structural fields and free text, inconsistent reference interpretation across formats/versions, and reuse of one bank credit for multiple invoices. Those concerns require deterministic parsing and replay controls, not permanent removal of the positive automatic path.

An example of a real reuse risk would be the same authenticated receipt being interpreted differently after a parser/profile change while its dedup key changes with the selected invoice reference. That requires an admissible alternate interpretation or a change in processing; it is not proved merely by showing a debtor can write a QR string. Rejecting conflicting fields and keeping source-credit identity independent of the matched invoice directly addresses it.

## Minimal viable parser

1. Recognize only an explicitly versioned, witnessed plain-text profile. Anchor from the beginning through the complete known ordered prefix; no search for a convenient header/reference later in the body. Require the known greeting/header shape, explicit credited direction, positive bounded decimal amount, currency, checksum-valid receiving IBAN and all three balance rows in their witnessed order.
2. Before the reference, allow only literal bank labels plus tightly bounded numeric/code grammar. A description restricted to a witnessed transfer code and numeric bank/account notation cannot contain a payer name, newline, extra field label or an embedded fake reference. Do not permit arbitrary text or skipped optional lines there. Expand codes/profiles only with evidence; current official code documentation can support a bounded value variation without proving every email layout.
3. Read one complete reference field at that structural position. A legitimate payer-controlled value in it is acceptable. Support the witnessed structured VS syntax; a bounded direct QR/E2E value can be a declared generic field-value variation, but its actual bank rendering remains a coverage qualification rather than something proved by synthetic substitution. Do not infer it from subject text or an arbitrary later note. Preserve exact/canonically specified characters; do not silently conflate references unless the provider contract guarantees that normalization.
4. Delimit subsequent free fields explicitly. Reject duplicate/reordered/reserved bank labels, forged prefix blocks and ambiguous references in free text instead of allowing the note to close a field and reopen bank structure. No missing-reference fallback. A counterparty account is never the receiving account.
5. Preserve MIME/line boundaries before structural recognition. For text/plain, decode the actual MIME transfer encoding once, reject controls/invalid encodings, and use only documented CRLF/whitespace normalization. Do not HTML-entity-decode or strip arbitrary tags to manufacture a plaintext template. HTML/table and multipart selection require their own provenance and adversarial qualification; an unsupported format can safely fall back while matching plain-text formats confirm automatically.
6. Preserve generic company import metadata independently. Only recognized, origin-verified source proof may populate SEPA automation fields. Keep provider contract-v2 store/original-account, amount/currency and unknown-outcome gates.

This design does not require a bank guarantee that the template will never change. It requires an exact recognizer, a positive supported path and safe rejection of messages outside that path.

## Mailgun origin and content binding

The selected ingress is Mailgun. Its callback authentication is not proof that the original sender was Tatra. Official documentation describes a DKIM Pass/Fail MIME header and newer authentication results in URL-route posts, but the reviewed public contract does not establish per-signature bank-domain binding, attacker-header stripping/overwrite, or full-body verification policy. This is a missing trust contract, not a demonstrated Mailgun vulnerability. Do not trust an arbitrary Pass header or invent a provider-owned attestation object. [Mailgun receiving filter documentation](https://documentation.mailgun.com/docs/mailgun/user-manual/receive-forward-store/spam-filter), [Mailgun's May 2026 authentication-results release](https://www.mailgun.com/releases/authentication-results-in-inbound-route-url-posts/).

The defensible implementation path is authenticated raw-MIME intake plus a pinned maintained DKIM/MIME library. Verify an actual bank-aligned signature against original bytes, reject partial-body `l=` coverage, and derive the parsed part from those same bytes. Require unique unambiguous From and relevant MIME identities, protect MIME interpretation headers, and bind the store recipient to the authenticated envelope/profile. A qualifying bank signature may coexist with other signatures when selection is explicit and bounded; global single-signature or mandatory optional timestamp rules are not fundamental requirements. Reject malformed, expired or unsupported cases according to an explicit policy. DKIM body-length limits can permit unsigned appended content and replay remains a separate problem. [RFC 6376 sections 3.5, 5.3.1, 8.2 and 8.6](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376).

A repository-local process is sufficient; a sidecar or new public listener is not inherently needed. Use argv/stdin rather than shell interpolation, bounded bytes/signatures/DNS/process time, typed verified/invalid/retryable outcomes and minimal internal JSON. DNS temporary failures must retry rather than gain trust. Encrypt raw MIME before queue persistence; do not put raw email, verification keys, signatures or process output into logs/failed-job diagnostics. A successful test of parser logic alone cannot establish this ingress boundary.

## Replay and credit identity

Separate delivery identity, bank-credit identity and matched invoice identity. Current body/index/selected-reference-dependent hashing is not a bank-issued transaction identity. A changed renderer or parser must not turn one receipt into another credit.

Prefer a bank movement ID if one is actually supplied. For the bounded historical subset, a conservative fingerprint of canonical bank-owned header/time/amount/currency/receiving IBAN, three balances and numeric counterparty description can be used without inventing a bank ID. Exclude the matched reference, free remittance, raw formatting, delivery ID and row index. Keep its schema stable across equivalent supported templates. It is a fingerprint: legitimate collisions or uncertain equivalence require review rather than a second settlement. Do not promise universal bank-level exactly-once from minute timestamps or Message-ID alone.

The existing plugin's stable invoice-reference recording still protects exact replay to the same invoice. Its cross-invoice Duplicate outcome blocks recording but does not prove the second invoice is paid; callers must preserve that distinction. Retain same-reference recovery after an external success/local-commit interruption. Changes in identity rules require explicit historical-replay handling, not silent reassignment of old receipts.

## Acceptance and actual remaining evidence

Source implementation and deterministic synthetic testing may proceed now. Required positive evidence is a genuinely passing synthetic bank DKIM signature plus a supported template that reaches one scoped provider report and settles once through the existing provider contract. Include complete positive tests; a suite proving only that all email is rejected is insufficient.

Negative cases must cover altered body/From, attacker signatures or fake Pass headers, partial-body signatures, duplicate identities/ambiguous MIME, DNS temporary failure, wrong original IBAN/currency/amount, debit, malformed prefix, fake labels in every free field, duplicate/reordered fields, missing reference, HTML/entity line injection and conflicting references. Replay tests must vary formatting/delivery/index/reference interpretation while preserving the source credit, exercise concurrent retries and ensure no second invoice is marked paid. Unknown outcomes remain review-only.

Operational gates are a working raw-MIME Mailgun route, the actual origin-verification implementation and production runtime dependencies/configuration, plus observed current-message coverage before claiming the user's present bank format is supported. No current local EML exists, so current-format/MIME/signature compatibility is not yet verified. That absence is not a reason to restore a broad regex, to abandon the automatic product, or to block preparation of the bounded implementation. It is a precise coverage limitation to close with the first structurally faithful current sample or normal owner-controlled operation.
